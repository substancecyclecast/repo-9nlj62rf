### THE PROBLEM

15,000+ crypto organizations manage $200B+ in treasuries using Google Sheets. They pay contractors manually across 40+ countries, have no real accounting, waste $25-50 per SWIFT transfer, and leave millions idle earning 0%.

### THE SOLUTION

**Mandate** is an autonomous CFO agent. Upload a CSV → in 90 seconds the AI routes every payment to the cheapest chain, screens compliance, settles via multisig, and produces auditor-ready books.
