# Mandate — Go-to-Market Plan

---

## Executive Summary

Land in DAO/crypto-native treasury market via accelerator networks (Alliance DAO), expand through Safe/Squads app ecosystems, then bridge to enterprise and traditional fintech customers.

**Year 1 target:** 50 paying orgs, $1M+ ARR.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Month 0-3)

### Objective: First 5-10 paying customers

#### Strategy: Accelerator + Community-Led Growth

**Actions:**
1. **Alliance DAO application** (top priority)
   - Demo: 15 contractors paid in 0.05s across 4 networks
   - Stellar angle: EMEA-focused, $0.00001/tx, 67% cheaper
   - Mentors ARE the users (they run DAO treasuries)

2. **Stellar Development Foundation**
   - Apply for Stellar Community Fund grant ($50K-$150K)
   - List on Stellar ecosystem page
   - Partnership with anchor providers (Cowrie, Flutterwave)

3. **Direct outreach to DAOs**
   - Target: 50 DAOs with $1M+ treasury and >10 contributors
   - Channels: Twitter DMs, Discord, governance forums
   - Pitch: "I can save you 67% on contractor payments and give you auditor-ready books"

4. **Content marketing**
   - "How we paid 15 contractors in 0.05 seconds" (Loom video)
   - "Why your DAO is overpaying for payroll" (blog post)
   - "Stellar vs SWIFT: real numbers" (comparison thread)

5. **Deploy live demo**
   - Public URL: app.mandate.finance
   - Sandbox mode — anyone can try without keys
   - CTA: "Try with your own CSV"

#### Target Customer Profiles (Phase 1):

| Type | Treasury | Contributors | Pain | Channels |
|------|----------|-------------|------|----------|
| Protocol DAO | $5-50M | 20-100 | Manual payroll, no books | Governance forums |
| Grants DAO | $1-10M | 50-200 | Cross-border complexity | Discord, Twitter |
| Dev shop | $1-5M | 10-50 | Multiple chains, compliance | Twitter, conferences |
| Investment DAO | $10-100M | 5-20 | Yield optimization, reporting | Direct/warm intro |

#### Pricing (Phase 1 — early adopter):
- **Starter:** $500/mo (up to 3 wallets, 20 payees)
- **Growth:** $1,000/mo (up to 10 wallets, 100 payees)
- Plus take rate: 0.15% on volume
- **Offer:** 3 months free for first 10 customers (LOI required)

## Phase 2: Growth (Month 3-6)

### Objective: 15-30 paying orgs, Safe/Squads distribution

#### Strategy: Platform Distribution

**Actions:**
1. **Safe App Store listing**
   - 6,000+ organizations, $100B+ in safes
   - Integration: Mandate as a Safe module/app
   - Feature: one-click import of Safe balances into Mandate

2. **Squads marketplace (Solana)**
   - Direct integration with Squads multisig
   - Solana DAO ecosystem (Marinade, Jupiter, Raydium)

3. **Partnerships:**
   - **Stellar anchors** (Cowrie for Nigeria, Flutterwave for Africa, settlement partners for EU)
   - **Yield protocols** (Morpho, Ondo) — co-marketing
   - **Compliance** (Chainalysis, Elliptic) — integration announcement

4. **Developer relations:**
   - Open-source agent toolkit
   - API documentation + SDKs
   - Hackathon sponsorships (ETHGlobal, Stellar hackathons)

5. **Case studies:**
   - Publish 3-5 customer case studies
   - Metrics: "Saved $X/month", "Reduced payroll time from 4h to 90s"
   - Video testimonials from treasurers

#### Pricing adjustment (if needed):
- Introduce annual plan (20% discount)
- Enterprise tier ($2,000/mo) for orgs with >$10M treasury
- Usage-based pricing for high-volume users

---

## Phase 3: Scale (Month 6-12)

### Objective: 50+ paying orgs, $1M ARR, prepare for seed

#### Strategy: Enterprise + Expansion

**Actions:**
1. **Enterprise sales:**
   - Target: crypto companies with $50M+ treasury
   - Hire 1 BD person (comp: $80K + commission)
   - Direct sales cycle: 2-4 weeks for $2K+/mo deals

2. **Product expansion:**
   - Mobile app (signature approvals on-the-go)
   - Direct bank integration (off-ramp without Bridge)
   - Multi-currency accounting (USD, EUR, GBP)
   - Tax lot reporting (US, EU)

3. **Geographical expansion:**
   - Open UK/EU entity for MiCA compliance
   - UAE entity for MENA market
   - Local anchor partnerships in 10+ new countries

4. **Thought leadership:**
   - Speak at conferences (ETHDenver, Token2049, Stellar Meridian)
   - Publish "State of Crypto Treasury" annual report
   - Advisory board: CFOs from top DAOs

5. **Fundraising preparation:**
   - Metrics package: ARR, NRR, GPV, CAC, LTV
   - Data room ready
   - Seed round: $3-5M at $20-30M valuation

## Risks & Contingencies

| Risk | Contingency |
|------|-------------|

| Regulatory changes | Multi-jurisdiction approach, pivot to compliant structures |
