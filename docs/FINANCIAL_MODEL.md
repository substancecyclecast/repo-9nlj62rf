## 7. Key Assumptions

1. **Crypto treasury market grows 30% YoY** (conservative given 2024-2025 growth)
2. **Average treasury size $2-5M** for target segment
3. **Net revenue retention 150%+** as orgs grow and deploy more capital
4. **Churn <2%/mo** due to high switching costs (double-entry ledger is system of record)
5. **No paid ads** in Year 1 — organic/community growth only
6. **Stellar adoption accelerates** with MiCA implementation (2025-2026)
7. **GPV per org grows** as trust builds (start with small batches, scale up)

## 8. Risk Factors & Mitigations

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|-----------|
| Regulatory change | Medium | High | Multi-jurisdiction, MiCA-first approach |
| Stellar network issues | Low | Medium | Multi-chain fallback (EVM + Solana) |
| Competition from Safe/Den | Medium | Medium | AI agent + cross-border = unique moat |
| Slow enterprise sales | High | Medium | Start with SMB DAOs, land-and-expand |
| Key person risk | Medium | High | Document everything, attract co-founder |
| Crypto winter | Medium | High | Revenue from SaaS (not token-dependent) |
